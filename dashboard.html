      MDP Project

      Smart Plant Health Monitoring & Automatic Irrigation

      A closed-loop system that measures, decides and acts. It waters the plant only when the measured conditions show that watering is needed, and lets you check on it and take over from anywhere.

      ESP32 Sensors Automated watering Plant health insights

      The problem

      Why smart irrigation is needed

      Overwatering

      Too much water can damage roots, waste resources and raise the risk of fungal problems.

      Underwatering

      Plants get stressed when watering follows a fixed schedule instead of the soil's actual state.

      No continuous feedback

      Checking by hand gives no continuous picture of soil and environmental conditions.
